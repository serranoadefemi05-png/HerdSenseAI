# HerdSense AI

### The intelligence layer for livestock.

HerdSense AI is an AI + IoT livestock intelligence platform designed to connect livestock, sensor data, telemetry, analytics, and farmer decision-making into one intelligent layer.

The goal is simple:

> **Turn livestock data into actionable intelligence.**

---

## 🐄 The Problem

Livestock represents significant economic value, yet farmers often have limited continuous visibility into the condition and behaviour of individual animals.

Important signals such as:

- Temperature
- Movement and activity
- Location
- Feeding and intake patterns
- Environmental conditions
- Telemetry
- Historical animal data

can exist as disconnected pieces of information.

HerdSense AI is being developed to bring these signals together and transform them into useful intelligence for livestock management.

---
